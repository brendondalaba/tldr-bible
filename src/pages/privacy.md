Bible Sidekick ("Bible Sidekick," "we," "us") is a free Bible reading tracker at bible-sidekick.netlify.app. This policy explains what information we handle, why, and the choices you have. If you have questions, email **[bible-sidekick@proton.me](mailto:bible-sidekick@proton.me)**.

## The short version

- You can use Bible Sidekick without an account. Your progress then stays in your browser and is never sent to us.
- If you sign in with Google, we store your name, email address and your reading progress so it syncs across your devices.
- We don't sell your information, show ads, or use analytics or tracking cookies.
- You can delete your account and all of your data at any time from the [Account](/account) page.

## Information we collect

### If you don't sign in

Your reading progress (which chapters you've checked and when) and your light/dark theme choice are saved in your browser's local storage on your device. This information is not sent to us, and we can't see it. Clearing your browser's site data erases it.

### If you sign in with Google

When you choose **Continue with Google**, Google shares basic profile information with us: your **name**, **email address**, **profile picture link**, and a **Google account identifier**. We only request this basic profile information; we do not request access to your Gmail, contacts, calendar, files, or any other Google data.

While you're signed in, we also store your **reading progress**:

- the chapters you mark as read and the date and time you marked each one, and
- each time you finish a book and start reading it again.

To keep you signed in, a session token is saved in your browser's local storage.

### Technical information

Like any website, the companies that host Bible Sidekick receive technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested. They use it to deliver the site, keep it secure and troubleshoot problems, and keep it in server logs for a limited time under their own policies.

## How we use your information

We use your information only to:

- create and run your account, and show your name and email on your Account page;
- save and sync your reading progress across your devices;
- keep the service secure and working; and
- respond to you if you contact us.

We do not sell, rent or trade your information. We do not use it for advertising, and we do not share it with anyone except the service providers below, who process it on our behalf to run Bible Sidekick.

## Google user data

Bible Sidekick's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements. We use the information Google shares only to sign you in and identify your account, and never to serve ads or for any purpose unrelated to Bible Sidekick.

## Service providers

- **Supabase**: stores accounts and reading progress, and handles sign-in. Data is stored in the United States.
- **Netlify**: hosts the website.
- **Google**: provides Google sign-in.

Each provider has its own privacy policy and handles data under its terms with us.

## How long we keep it

We keep your account information and reading progress for as long as you have an account. When you delete your account, your account and reading progress are erased from our database immediately. Copies may remain in our providers' routine backups or logs for a short time until those are overwritten.

## Your choices and rights

- **See your data:** your name, email and reading progress are shown in the app on your Account page and book pages.
- **Delete your data:** use **Delete my account** on the [Account](/account) page. It permanently erases your account and all of your reading progress. To clear progress saved while signed out, clear this site's data in your browser.
- **Sign out** at any time from the Account page.
- **Disconnect Google:** you can also remove Bible Sidekick's access from your [Google Account permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).
- **Questions or requests:** email [bible-sidekick@proton.me](mailto:bible-sidekick@proton.me). We'll respond within 30 days.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights, such as to access, correct or receive a copy of your information. Email us and we'll help.

## Children

Accounts are for people **13 and older**. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, email us and we'll delete it. Younger readers can use Bible Sidekick without an account, with a parent's guidance; their progress then stays on their device.

## Security

We protect your information with encrypted connections (HTTPS) and database rules that let each account see and change only its own reading progress. No method of storing or sending data is completely secure, but we work to protect yours.

## Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the "Last updated" date above. If a change is significant, we'll also note it on the site.
